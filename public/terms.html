Last updated: August 2026
Before you read this

stackTrace is a personal learning project, not a company. These terms exist so the ground rules for using it are written down somewhere, not because there's a legal team behind them.

These terms cover creating an account, posting content, and using the vote and comment features on stackTrace.

01

Accounts

You need an account to post, comment, or vote. You're responsible for whatever is posted from your account. Use a real-looking username if you like, but don't impersonate a specific real person.

02

Your content

Whatever you post — a post, a comment, a reply — is still yours. Posting it here just means it's visible to anyone browsing the feed, and stored in the app's data files so it can be shown back to you and others.

You can delete your own posts and comments at any time.

03

Acceptable use

Keep it to what the app is actually for: sharing developer-focused writing and discussion. Don't:

  • Post anything illegal, harassing, or intended to harm someone
  • Try to break the app's auth, vote counting, or data storage on purpose
  • Scrape or spam the feed with automated posts
04

Voting and comments

Upvotes and downvotes are meant to reflect one honest vote per person, per post or comment. Vote counts are computed from real vote records, not editable by hand.

05

Availability

This app may be hosted on free-tier infrastructure. That means it can go to sleep after a period of inactivity (a first request afterward may take a little while to respond), and it may restart or reset at any time. Don't rely on it staying up, or on data staying there forever.

06

No warranty

This app is provided as-is, as a learning project, with no guarantee it will always work correctly, stay available, or keep your data safe. Use it accordingly — nothing important should depend on it.

07

Termination

An account that's used to spam, harass, or abuse the app can be removed at any time.

08

Changes to these terms

If these terms change as the project grows, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change with it.

09

Contact

Questions about these terms can go through the project's repository — see the link in the site footer.
