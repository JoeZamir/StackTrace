Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
Before you read this

stackTrace is a personal learning project built to practice backend fundamentals — it isn't a commercial product with a legal or security team behind it. This page describes how the app actually handles data today, plainly, rather than reading like boilerplate copied from somewhere else.

This policy explains what information stackTrace collects when you create an account or use the feed, how it's stored, and what control you have over it.

01

What information is collected

Creating an account collects:

  • Full name, email address, and username, as you type them into the signup form
  • A password, used only to verify it's you when you log back in

Using the feed, without an account, collects nothing — browsing posts and comments is anonymous.

Posting, commenting, and voting are tied to your account so the feed can show who wrote what and reflect your own vote state back to you.

02

How it's stored

Account and content data is stored in plain data files on the server — there's no third-party database provider, analytics vendor, or ad network involved anywhere in this app.

Honest limitation: passwords are currently stored as plain text on the server, not hashed. This is a known gap in an in-progress learning project, not an oversight you're meant to trust. Please don't reuse a password here that you use anywhere that matters.

03

How it's used

Your information is used only to:

  • Let you log in and be recognized as the author of your own posts, comments, and votes
  • Show your display name and initials next to what you post
  • Let you edit or delete your own content

Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or sent to any third party. There are no third parties integrated into this app.

04

Cookies and local storage

stackTrace doesn't use cookies or tracking scripts. It stores one small piece of information in your browser's local storage — a note saying which account is currently logged in on this device — so you don't have to log in again every time you reload the page. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

05

Your controls

You can edit your profile information, delete your own posts and comments, and log out at any time, which removes the local sign-in note from your browser.

06

Security — read this one

This is a demo/learning project, not a production service with a security review behind it. Treat any account you create here as low-stakes: use a throwaway password, and don't post anything sensitive in a comment or bio field.

07

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes as the project grows, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change with it.

08

Contact

Questions about this policy can go through the project's repository — see the link in the site footer.
